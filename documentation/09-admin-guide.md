# Admin guide

## People

Open **People & permissions**.

- **Invite:** choose a role and send. The link lasts 72 hours; use **Resend invite** if it expires. Five people can be invited or active at once.
- **Change a role or payroll access:** pick a new value. That person is signed out and signs in again.
- **Deactivate:** they lose access at once and their seat is freed. Their history stays. **Reactivate** brings them back if a seat is free.
- There is always at least one admin.

Payroll access is separate from role. Granting it, even to yourself, is recorded.

## Documents

- Upload from the organisation's **Document library** or a grant's **Documents** tab.
- If AI suggests dates, check them against the quote, change or clear them, tick the box and confirm. If AI cannot read the file, type the dates.
- Open a document to replace its file, edit its details or archive it. Old versions are kept.

## Grants and deadlines

- Add a grant, then upload its contract and choose **Read with AI**. Untick any deadline you do not want before confirming.
- Give every deadline and document an owner. Items without an active owner appear under Needs attention, but nobody is emailed.

## Alerts

Emails go out once a day: documents 30 days before their date and when overdue; deadlines at their chosen reminder and when overdue. Each is sent once. If one email fails, the run stops and the rest go out on the next run, so fix a failing address promptly.

## Audit log

Every upload, approval and edit, with who and when. Open an entry to see what changed. Entries cannot be edited or deleted. Payroll entries are visible only to admins with payroll access.
